## Question
Which destinations / segments drive group applications?

## Answer
No single destination dominates — volume is fairly evenly spread across 14 destinations that appear in `group_started` (n=1,200), from Thailand (112 starts, 57 submitted, 50.89% conversion) down to Japan (70 starts, 40 submitted, 57.14%). Destination conversion-to-`group_submitted` ranges 50.89%–64.63% (Indonesia highest at 64.63%, n=82), a real but modest ~14pp spread — no destination stands out as a strong outlier either way.

The real driver is **`group_size`, not destination or any other segment**: smaller groups both dominate volume and convert far better. 2-traveller groups are the largest segment (475/1,200 = 39.6% of starts) *and* convert best (69.47% to `group_submitted`); conversion falls monotonically as group size grows — 3-pax 58.66%, 4-pax 50.42%, 5-pax 38.60%, 6-pax just 31.11%. That's a 38pp spread from smallest to largest group, dwarfing every other cut tested.

Other segments are flat by comparison: `device_type` (ios 484 starts/55.99%, android 395/59.24%, web-user-b2c 231/56.28%, Desktop 90/58.89%) shows no meaningful gap. `geoip_country_code` is dominated by `IN` (726/1,200 = 60.5% of starts, 57.71% conversion) simply because the platform's user base is India-heavy — treat this as a volume-concentration fact, not evidence that Indian users are disproportionately more likely to start a group application, since no baseline (share of all users who are `IN`) was checked here.

**Scope:** `group_started`/`group_submitted`, the Group/Family spec 02 sample, 1,200 groups / 688 submissions, window 2026-06-08 → 2026-06-28. Per known_issues.md D2, this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started` — the group flow cannot be joined to the main funnel or to platform-wide destination/geo baselines, so all figures here are standalone shares *within the group flow itself*, not conversion-rate-vs-the-rest-of-the-funnel comparisons. Conversion is computed by set-membership (`group_submitted.group_id` is a subset of `group_started.group_id` by construction, so this join is safe per D1) rather than `windowFunnel`.
