## Question
Completion rate (group_started → group_submitted) by group size — where do large groups fall off?

## Answer
Completion rate falls **monotonically as group size grows**, more than halving from the smallest to the largest groups:

| `group_size` | Groups started | Groups submitted | Completion rate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 475 | 330 | **69.47%** |
| 3 | 283 | 166 | **58.66%** |
| 4 | 238 | 120 | **50.42%** |
| 5 | 114 | 44 | **38.60%** |
| 6 | 90 | 28 | **31.11%** |

(Totals reconcile to the spec-level figures: 1,200 groups started / 688 submitted = 57.33% overall.) Large groups (5–6 travellers) are where the flow leaks hardest — completion is roughly **38pp lower** for 6-person groups than for 2-person groups, a steady, size-proportional decline rather than a cliff at one particular size.

A likely contributing mechanism: `traveller_removed` (mid-flow churn, before submit) also scales sharply with group size — only 0.42% of size-2 groups ever have a removal event, vs. **18.4% of size-5 and 17.8% of size-6 groups**, and average travellers added per group grows roughly with group size (1.91 at size 2 up to 5.04 at size 6). Bigger groups require coordinating more co-traveller entries and are more likely to churn a member before submitting — consistent with, though not proof of, the completion-rate gap. Note this dataset only instruments two funnel moments for this flow (`group_started` and `group_submitted`, plus the fan-out `traveller_added`/`traveller_removed` events) — there is no intermediate step (e.g. "docs entered", "review reached") to say more precisely *which step* inside the flow large groups abandon at, only that they abandon more, and abandon more often after visible mid-flow churn.

**Method and scope:** computed by set membership (`group_submitted.group_id ⊆ group_started.group_id` by construction, per known_issues.md D1/D2 guidance for this table — no `windowFunnel` used, and no reliance on the `application_id` join, which is independently confirmed at 0% overlap against `application_started` for all 4 Group/Family tables). `group_size` taken from `group_started` (the size chosen at group creation), not `group_submitted.travellers_submitted` (the size at submit time), since the question is about the size a group *started* with. Sample: 1,200 groups in `group_started`, 688 in `group_submitted`, window 2026-06-08 → 2026-06-28 — the only Group/Family data currently loaded; treat as directional, not a full-population estimate.
