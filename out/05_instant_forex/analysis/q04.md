## Question
Which destinations / currencies attach best; any segment (device/geo) skew?

## Answer
Attach rate = `forex_purchased` ÷ `forex_offer_shown`, joined on `user_id` (safe here — both tables are 1-row-per-user, D6). This is a two-step funnel, so per D1 it was computed by set membership, not `windowFunnel`: unlike the main visa funnel, this one **is** cleanly monotonic — all 546 matched `user_id`s have `forex_purchased.timestamp ≥ forex_offer_shown.timestamp` (546/546, 100%), so the row-count ratio the wiki previously flagged as unverified (18.83%) is now confirmed as the correct number (546/2,900 = **18.83%** overall), not an artifact.

**By destination** (14 observed, n=174–240 offers each): best **US** 24.58% (58/236), SG 23.12%, TH 22.87%, MY 21.76%, FR 20.41% — worst **AU** 13.78% (27/196), VN 14.29%, TR 14.78%. ~11pp spread top-to-bottom.

**By `to_currency`**: tracks destination almost exactly, with one wrinkle — **EUR** (18.81%, n=436) is a blend of two Eurozone destinations, FR (20.41%, n=196) and GR (17.5%, n=240), since `to_currency` is coarser than `destination` wherever two destinations share a currency. Report currency-level attach rates with this caveat rather than treating EUR as a single market — it masks a 3pp destination-level gap. All other currencies map 1:1 to a destination (USD↔US, THB↔TH, etc.).

**Device skew — mild**: ios best (19.77%, n=1,234), android 18.82%, Desktop 17.55%, web-user-b2c worst (17.08%, n=527). ~2.7pp spread, and per-cell n is small enough (188–1,234) that this is directional, not a strong platform effect.

**Geo skew — mild-to-moderate**: AE best (21.51%, n=279), SG 19.87%, IN 19.01% (India is 61.7% of all offers shown — 1,789/2,900 — so the overall 18.83% figure is effectively India-weighted), AU 17.89%, SA 17.71%, US 15.25%, GB worst (15.11%, n=139). ~6.4pp spread across only 7 observed geos (not all 9 platform-wide country codes appear in this sample).

**Caveats**: sample is `out/05_instant_forex` only, 2026-06-08→2026-06-28, 2,900 offer-shown rows / 546 purchases — small per-cell counts (96–436) mean individual destination/currency/geo rankings have wide margins; treat gaps under ~5pp as directional, not conclusive. Per known_issues.md D2, this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started`, so this flow cannot be joined to the main visa funnel, insurance/plan add-ons, or other specs — every figure above is scoped to the standalone forex add-on flow.
