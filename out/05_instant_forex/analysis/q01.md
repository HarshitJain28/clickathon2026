## Question
Attach rate: offer_shown → forex_purchased, overall and by `destination`.

## Answer
**Overall attach rate: 18.83%** (546 of 2,900 users who saw `forex_offer_shown` went on to have a matching row in `forex_purchased`). This is a live, verified set-membership join on `user_id` (`forex_offer_shown.user_id ⊇ forex_purchased.user_id`, exact match, no fan-out — both tables are confirmed 1 row per user: 2,900 rows/2,900 distinct users and 546 rows/546 distinct users respectively), not a `windowFunnel`/ratio estimate (per known_issues.md D1). It exactly reproduces the previously-unverified row-count ratio noted in `tables/forex_purchased.md` (546/2,900 = 18.83%), so that figure is now confirmed rather than merely a ratio coincidence.

**Scope/caveats:** Instant Forex sample window 2026-06-08 06:00 → 2026-06-28 23:12 (n=2,900 offers shown, n=546 purchases) — a 3-week sample, not the full H1 2026 window. Per `known_issues.md` D2, this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started` (verified 2026-08-02), so this flow cannot be joined to the main funnel or reported as a rate over applications/card-clicks — it is analysed standalone, on its own `offer_shown` denominator, as intended. Per-destination sample sizes are small (174–240 shown users each), so per-destination attach rates should be read as directional rather than precise to the decimal.
