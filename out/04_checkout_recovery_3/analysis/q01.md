## Question
Reconversion (recovery) rate by **drop_step** — which step is most recoverable?

## Answer
Recovery rate is roughly flat across the first three drop steps (~4.5–4.8%) and clearly worst for the earliest step:

| `drop_step` | Abandoned (`abandonment_detected`) | Reconverted | Recovery rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `application_started` | 521 | 25 | **4.80%** |
| `pay_now_clicked` | 397 | 19 | 4.79% |
| `document_uploaded` | 696 | 31 | 4.45% |
| `destination_card_clicked` | 686 | 18 | **2.62%** |
| **Overall** | **2,300** | **93** | **4.04%** |

`application_started` and `pay_now_clicked` are effectively tied for most recoverable (4.80% vs 4.79%, on n=521/397 — a 1-user swing would flip the ranking, so treat this as a tie rather than a real ordering). `document_uploaded` sits close behind at 4.45%. `destination_card_clicked` — the earliest, lowest-intent drop point — is clearly the *least* recoverable, at roughly half the rate of the other three (2.62%).

**Method / caveats:**
- Computed by verified set-membership join on `user_id` (not the row-count ratios in `tables/reconverted.md`/`abandonment_detected.md`, which per known-issue D1 are unverified until a live query runs — this is that query). 100% of `reconverted` rows' `user_id` (93/93) are a subset of `abandonment_detected`'s, and for every matched user the `drop_step` recorded on `reconverted` agrees exactly with the `drop_step` recorded on `abandonment_detected` (93/93) — the per-user recovery attribution is internally consistent, not just a copied label.
- Overall rate (93/2,300 = 4.04%) matches the wiki's cited headline figure exactly, and both `abandonment_detected` (2,300 rows) and `reconverted` (93 rows) came back at their documented row counts on a live `count()` — the open "was spec 04 double-loaded by the `04_checkout_recovery_3` resubmission" question in `known_issues.md` → D2 does **not** show up as duplication in the live data for these two tables; counts are singular, not doubled.
- Per D2, this spec's `application_id` still does not join `application_started` (0% overlap) — this analysis stays entirely within the 6 standalone spec-04 tables via `user_id`, as required.
- Sample: full live spec-04 table contents, window 2026-06-08 06:01 → 2026-07-01 00:00. n is small per step (397–696 abandoned, 18–31 reconverted) — treat the ranking as directional, especially the near-tie at the top.
