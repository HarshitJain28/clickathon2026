## Question
Which **channel** recovers best (open → click → reconvert)?

## Answer
**Push** recovers best end-to-end, despite WhatsApp winning the earlier "open" step. Live set-membership join (on `user_id`, anchored to `reminder_sent`, against `reminder_opened`/`reminder_cta_clicked`/`reconverted` — not `windowFunnel`, per known_issues.md D1) over the 2,300-row Abandoned Checkout Recovery sample (2026-06-08 06:01 → 2026-07-01 00:00):

| Channel | Sent | Opened | Open rate | Clicked (of opened) | Reconverted | **Reconvert rate (of sent)** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| WhatsApp | 484 | 224 | **46.28%** (best) | 34.82% | 21 | 4.34% |
| **Push** | 1,138 | 322 | 28.30% | 40.99% | 53 | **4.66%** (best) |
| Email | 678 | 144 | 21.24% | 40.28% | 19 | 2.80% (worst on every step) |

WhatsApp attracts opens far better than the other two channels, but converts those opens into clicks and final reconversions at the lowest rate of the three (34.82% click-of-opened, 26.9% reconvert-of-clicked). Push has the weakest open rate but the strongest click-through and reconvert-through, ending up with the best overall recovery-of-sent rate (4.66% vs. WhatsApp's 4.34%); email trails on every single step and every channel's `channel` value is 100% internally consistent across the send→open leg (verified: 690/690 `reminder_opened` rows carry the same channel as their matching `reminder_sent` row), so this isn't an artifact of channel mixing between stages.

**Scope/caveats:** this re-runs the K5 (WhatsApp nudge) test with an actual live query — the numbers above turn out to be identical to the row-count ratios previously flagged in `tables/reminder_sent.md` as "unverified," so that spec's directional read is now confirmed by real join, not coincidence. Sample is small at the tail (21–53 reconversions per channel over ~3 weeks) — a half-point push/WhatsApp gap on n≈20-50 is not a large margin. This spec's `application_id` has a verified 0% overlap against `application_started` (D2) so it cannot be joined to the main funnel; all figures here are standalone within the recovery flow's own 6 tables, joined on `user_id` (safe — every user appears at most once per table, D6 holds). Also verified live: current row counts (`reminder_sent`=2,300, `reminder_opened`=690, `reminder_cta_clicked`=268, `reconverted`=93, `abandonment_detected`=2,300, `resumed_at_step`=268) exactly match the wiki's documented figures with no duplication — the open "duplicate-load" question flagged in known_issues.md → D2 does not appear to have doubled these tables as of this query.
