## Question
Does timing (`hours_since_drop`) matter — send at 1h vs 24h vs 48h?

## Answer
No — timing has no meaningful effect. `reminder_sent.hours_since_drop` takes 5 values (1, 3, 6, 24, 48h; 2,300 rows total, 100% populated). Joining `reminder_sent → reminder_opened → reminder_cta_clicked → reconverted` on `user_id` (safe here: D6 confirms 1 row/user per table, and the join totals reproduce each downstream table's full row count exactly — 690 opens, 268 clicks, 93 reconversions — with no fan-out, so this is a verified set-membership join, not a row-count-ratio guess):

| Sent at | Sent | Opened | Open rate | Clicked | Clicked (of sent) | Reconverted | Recovery rate (of sent) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1h | 433 | 141 | 32.56% | 49 | 11.32% | 15 | **3.46%** |
| 3h | 484 | 135 | 27.89% | 53 | 10.95% | 20 | **4.13%** |
| 6h | 496 | 153 | 30.85% | 60 | 12.10% | 24 | **4.84%** |
| 24h | 469 | 141 | 30.06% | 52 | 11.09% | 17 | **3.62%** |
| 48h | 418 | 120 | 28.71% | 54 | 12.92% | 17 | **4.07%** |
| **Overall** | **2,300** | **690** | 30.00% | **268** | 11.65% | **93** | **4.04%** |

Recovery rate ranges only 3.46%–4.84% across all five send delays, with no monotonic pattern by hour (6h is numerically best, 1h numerically worst, but 24h and 48h land in between, not at the extremes you'd expect from a real "recency decays effectiveness" story). Given each bucket's overall recovery rate has a binomial standard error of ~0.9–1.0pp (pooled base rate 4.04%, n≈420–500 per bucket), every bucket's rate sits within ~1 SE of the overall mean — the spread is consistent with sampling noise, not a real timing effect. Open rate (27.9%–32.6%) and click-through (10.95%–12.92%) are similarly flat across all five delays.

**Scope/caveats:** spec 04 sample, 2026-06-08→2026-07-01, 2,300 abandonment→reminder pairs. This is the first verified (set-membership, not ratio) query run against this spec's 6-table chain — it also incidentally confirms `reminder_sent`'s live row count is 2,300, not 4,600, settling (for this table) the open duplicate-load question flagged in `known_issues.md` D2/`tables/index.md` for the `out/04_checkout_recovery_3` resubmission — no double-insert occurred. Per D2, `application_id` does not join to `application_started` (0% overlap) for this spec, so this analysis is standalone (recovery flow only, not tied to the main funnel). This answers timing, not channel — see `reminder_sent.md` for the (still row-count-ratio, unverified) channel breakdown, which is a separate question.
