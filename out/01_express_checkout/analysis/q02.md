## Question
Is there a platform where OTP / payment fails more (e.g. iOS)? Cut `otp_success` and confirmation rate by `device_type` / `os` / `geoip_country_code`.

## Answer
**Yes — iOS.** Every one of the 70 OTP failures in the Express Checkout sample (`otp_entered`, 1,007 rows, 2026-06-08→2026-06-28) occurred on `device_type = 'ios'` / `os = 'iOS'`. All other platforms show **100% `otp_success`**:

| device_type | n | otp_success rate | confirmed (`express_payment_confirmed`) | confirmation rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **ios** | 428 | **83.64%** | 316 | **73.83%** |
| android | 338 | 100% | 303 | 89.64% |
| web-user-b2c | 185 | 100% | 170 | 91.89% |
| Desktop | 56 | 100% | 47 | 83.93% |

The `os` column (`iOS` n=428) matches `device_type='ios'` exactly, so both cuts agree — this is one real, concentrated effect, not a casing artifact (see D9). By `geoip_country_code`, the pattern is volume-weighted by where iOS traffic sits rather than a distinct geo effect: SG (89.36% success, n=94) and AE (92.05%, n=88) are the weakest geos, but IN carries most of the volume (606 rows, 93.56% success) and no country shows the near-total failure concentration that `device_type`/`os` do — device/OS is the driver, not geography.

Digging one level further: conditional on `otp_success = true`, iOS's confirmation rate recovers to 88.27% — in line with Android (89.64%) and above Desktop (83.93%). So the platform gap lives almost entirely **at the OTP step itself**, not in a separate downstream iOS payment-confirmation problem.

**Context for this finding — updates K1, doesn't contradict it.** `known_issues.md` K1 ("iOS WebKit OTP regression") was refuted on the *main* funnel's `pay_now_clicked → purchase_completed` step, where iOS actually converts **best** (49.88% vs Android 46.77%, and +27pp over Android in the UAE). That test had no OTP-specific columns and used a different table/population. `otp_entered.otp_success` is the first direct instrumentation of the OTP mechanism itself, and on it iOS is uniquely, consistently the failing platform (100% of failures, 0 failures anywhere else). Recommend the Context Agent record this as a **new, dated K1 re-test verdict**: OTP-step failures are real and iOS-concentrated in Express Checkout, even though the broader pay-step conversion story remains iOS-favorable.

**Scope/caveats:** small standalone sample (1,007 `otp_entered` rows / 836 confirmations, 3-week window, 2026-06-08 to 2026-06-28); per D2, this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started`, so it cannot be joined to the main funnel — this analysis stays within Express Checkout's own tables, joined `otp_entered` → `express_payment_confirmed` on `user_id` (safe 1:1 join per D6, no repeat users). Geo cells below n≈50 (SA, GB, AU) are directional only.
