## Question
Conversion lift: do coupon users reach `checkout_with_coupon` at a higher rate than the no-coupon baseline (rows where `coupon_code` is null)?

## Answer
No — the opposite is true. Coupon users reach `checkout_with_coupon` at a *lower* rate than the no-coupon baseline, not a higher one.

Computed by set-membership join on `user_id` across spec 06's own tables (D1-safe; per D2 this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started`, so the analysis is scoped standalone, not to the main funnel): of the 2,100 users who saw `coupon_field_shown`, 848 went on to `coupon_entered` and 1,252 never engaged with the coupon field at all. Of the 848 who entered a code, 580 were accepted (`coupon_applied`) and 268 were rejected (`coupon_rejected`) — and **every one of the 268 rejected users drops out completely**, never appearing in `checkout_with_coupon` in any form. Only 366 of the 848 coupon-entering users (**43.16%**) go on to reach `checkout_with_coupon` (all 366 with `coupon_code` populated there, and all drawn from the successful-`coupon_applied` subset — 366/580 = 63.10% of appliers convert, 0/268 rejects do). Meanwhile, 621 of the 1,252 users who never touched the coupon field (**49.60%**) reach `checkout_with_coupon` with `coupon_code` NULL. So the no-coupon baseline converts roughly **6.4 percentage points higher** (49.60% vs. 43.16%).

Scope/caveat: this is spec 06's own 2026-06-08→2026-06-28 sample (2,100 `coupon_field_shown` rows; 987 `checkout_with_coupon` rows, which split exactly into 366 coupon + 621 no-coupon with zero cross-contamination in either direction — a clean, non-fan-out set-membership partition, not a ratio estimate). Per known_issues.md D2, none of the 6 Promo/Coupon tables' `application_id` overlaps `application_started` (0%), so this finding cannot be generalized to the main visa-application funnel — it describes the coupon mini-flow only. The apparent "lift" reversal is driven almost entirely by coupon-rejection being a hard stop: rejected users show 0% downstream checkout completion in this sample.
