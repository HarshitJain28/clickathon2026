## Question
Segment cuts (device, geo, destination); which codes work where.

## Answer

Scope: Promo/Coupon spec (spec 06, sealed/unseen), `coupon_entered` (848 attempts, 2026-06-08→2026-06-28), anchored with a **live set-membership check** (not a row-count assumption) against `coupon_applied`/`coupon_rejected` on `user_id` — confirms the 580+268=848 partition flagged as "unverified" in the wiki is in fact an exact, non-overlapping split of every attempt (per `known_issues.md` D1, `application_id` is unjoinable to the main funnel for this spec — 0% overlap — so this is scoped to the coupon flow standalone). Overall success rate (applied ÷ entered) = 580/848 = **68.4%**.

**By coupon code, overall:** `EXPIRED5` is **0% everywhere** — it never succeeds in any device/geo/destination cut (149 attempts, 0 applied), consistent with it being a permanently-expired code rather than a segment-dependent failure. The other 5 live codes (`ATLYS15`, `FIRST10`, `FREESHIP`, `SUMMER20`, `WELCOME`) all cluster in a broad 60–100% success band per segment, with no code standing out as a clean universal winner or loser.

**By device_type (n=9–73 per cell, the most reliable cut):**

| Code | Desktop | android | ios | web-user-b2c |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ATLYS15 | 100% (n=9) | 69.2% (n=39) | 83.1% (n=65) | 81.5% (n=27) |
| FIRST10 | 84.6% (n=13) | 87.2% (n=47) | 81.7% (n=60) | 85.0% (n=20) |
| FREESHIP | **57.1%** (n=14) | 84.2% (n=38) | 87.7% (n=73) | 90.0% (n=30) |
| SUMMER20 | 83.3% (n=12) | 87.0% (n=54) | 86.0% (n=50) | 92.0% (n=25) |
| WELCOME | 66.7% (n=9) | 69.2% (n=39) | 81.5% (n=54) | 90.5% (n=21) |

`FREESHIP` is notably weaker on Desktop (57.1%, n=14) than everywhere else it appears (84–90%); `ATLYS15` and `WELCOME` both dip to 69.2% on android specifically (same rate, same n=39 — worth flagging as a pattern, not yet enough evidence to call it causal). `ios`/`web-user-b2c` are consistently the strongest device segments across codes.

**By geo (`geoip_country_code`) and destination:** volume concentrates heavily in `IN` (70–93 attempts per code, ~7–10× any other geo) with 78–91% success there for the 5 live codes — India is both the highest-volume and a solidly-performing geo for every working code. Outside India, per-code-per-geo cells drop to n=2–20, and per-code-per-destination cells (27 destinations × 6 codes) drop to n=4–19 — too thin to call individual code/destination pairs reliably (e.g. `WELCOME` reads weakest in `US`, 63.2%, but that's also its largest non-India cell at n=19; `ATLYS15` reads weakest in `SG`, 45.5%, n=11). Directionally, `SA`/`AU`/`GB` show more single-digit-n cells with wide swings (33%–100%) for several codes — noise, not a segment effect, at this sample size.

**Bottom line:** the one robust, high-confidence segment finding is that `EXPIRED5` fails in 100% of device/geo/destination cuts (by design, not by segment). Beyond that, device_type is the only cut with enough volume per cell to trust directionally — `FREESHIP` underperforms on Desktop and `ATLYS15`/`WELCOME` both underperform on android, while `ios`/`web-user-b2c` are the strongest device segments platform-wide. Geo and destination cuts are real but too sparse per code (most cells n<20) to say a given code "works" in a specific country or destination with confidence; India is the only geo with enough volume to trust its per-code rates. All figures are Promo/Coupon-spec-standalone (no `application_id` join to the main visa funnel — 0% overlap, per D2) and cover the 3-week 2026-06-08→2026-06-28 sample only.
