## Question
Share rate, and does it vary by `status_shared` (do approvals get shared more)?

## Answer
This can't be answered yet — the instrumentation it needs isn't live. I confirmed directly against the `clickathon` database (`list_tables` on all 17 tables, then a full column scan across every table) that no table or column contains "share" or "status" anywhere in its name or schema: the live tables are `destination_card_clicked`, `application_started`, `document_uploaded`, `pay_now_clicked`, `purchase_completed`, `search_typed`, `landing_page_scrolled`, `auth_completed`, the 5 Express Checkout tables, and the 4 Group/Family tables (`group_started`, `traveller_added`, `traveller_removed`, `group_submitted`) — none of them has a `status_shared` column, a `share_*` event, or anything resembling an approval-status field. The wiki's tables index and known_issues.md (checked in full) confirm the same 17-table inventory and don't reference a "share"/"approval" concept either — this appears to belong to a spec (e.g. a group/family approval-sharing feature) that hasn't been instrumented and loaded into this database yet.

To answer "share rate" and "does approval status affect share rate" once live, we'd need at minimum: an event marking an application/itinerary as shared (with a timestamp and an actor/recipient), plus the `status_shared` field (or equivalent approval-status column) on the shared entity, joinable via a stable key (per D2 in known_issues.md, watch for 32-char vs 36-char UUID `application_id` mismatches — that trap has broken every spec's join so far). Until that lands, no live number can be reported without fabricating one, so none is given here.
