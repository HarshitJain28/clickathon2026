Takeaways

  • Fix the big one first: 86.76% of started applications never upload a document — uniform across every segment cut here, so it's a UX/flow fix, not a segment‑targeted one.
  • The segment-sensitive leak is at payment: pay‑now → purchase swings ~9pp by geo (AE 56.2% vs SG 41.4%) and ~4pp by device (iOS 49.9% vs Android 46.4%). No known issue in the wiki explains it — K1 ("Gulf/iOS abandon at pay step") is refuted here too: iOS is the best-converting device, and AE the best-converting geo, both against K1's prediction.
  • Overall funnel conversion is declining ~20% relative across H1 (4.91% → 3.93%, per metrics/funnel_conversion.md), unexplained by K4 (refuted as Schengen‑specific). This decline is broader than any single segment shown here and worth flagging as an open question.
