## Question
Margin cost: total `discount_amount`; which codes drive volume vs erode margin.

## Answer
Source: `clickathon.coupon_applied` (580 rows, the "coupon successfully applied" event — 100% `discount_amount` populated, 5 distinct codes; sample 2026-06-08 → 2026-06-28). Per known-issue **D7**, `discount_amount` spans 7 currencies with no FX rate, so totals below are **grouped by currency**, not summed together; INR is the dominant currency (361 of 580 uses, 62.2%) and is used as the headline.

**INR-only breakdown (361 uses, ₹200,557 total discount):**

| Code | Uses (volume) | Total discount (₹) | Avg discount/use (₹) | Type |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| SUMMER20 | 82 | 87,088 | 1,062.05 | percent |
| FIRST10 | 78 | 41,639 | 533.83 | percent |
| FREESHIP | 78 | 0 | 0 | flat |
| ATLYS15 | 68 | 55,330 | 813.68 | percent |
| WELCOME | 55 | 16,500 | 300.00 (fixed) | flat |

**Volume vs. margin erosion don't line up 1:1:**
- **SUMMER20** is the single biggest cost driver on *both* axes — highest usage volume (82 uses, INR) and the highest total/average discount (₹87,088 total, ₹1,062.05/use). It is the clearest "erodes margin AND drives volume" code. (Note: per `known_issues.md` K6, SUMMER20 is an always-on code running all 6 months, not a seasonal campaign — its volume isn't a one-off spike.)
- **FREESHIP** ties for 2nd-highest volume (78 uses) but records **₹0** `discount_amount` on every single use, in every currency — it drives real volume at (recorded) zero margin cost. Worth flagging to the PM as either a genuine no-cost perk or a possible instrumentation gap (shipping cost may not be captured in this field).
- **ATLYS15** has the *second-lowest* volume (68 uses, only above WELCOME) but the *second-highest* total discount (₹55,330) — its high per-use average (₹813.68) means it erodes margin disproportionately to how often it's used.
- **FIRST10** sits in the middle on both volume and cost.
- **WELCOME** is cheapest and lowest-cost: a fixed flat ₹300/use, lowest volume, lowest total spend.

By currency, grand totals (all 5 codes combined, do not sum across rows): INR 200,557 / SGD 29,423 / USD 20,740 / AED 20,535 / AUD 14,490 / GBP 13,954 / SAR 11,742 (580 uses total, all currencies).

**Caveats:** (1) `checkout_with_coupon` (the downstream checkout-conversion event) carries its own independently-populated `coupon_code`/`discount_amount` for a different, smaller population (366 with-coupon rows vs. 580 here) and gives noticeably different per-code INR totals (e.g. SUMMER20 ₹47,262 vs. ₹87,088 here) — the two sources are **not reconciled** at the schema level (flagged on both tables' wiki pages); this answer uses `coupon_applied` as the "discount actually granted" source of truth. (2) Per known-issue **D2**, this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started` — these figures are scoped to the coupon flow standalone, not joined to the main funnel. (3) `EXPIRED5` (a 6th code) never appears in `coupon_applied` at all — it only ever reaches `coupon_rejected`, so it has zero volume and zero margin cost by construction.
