## Question
Coupon apply rate (field_shown → coupon_applied) and valid vs rejected mix; top reject reasons.

## Answer
Over the coupon flow's full sample (2,100 `coupon_field_shown` events, 2026-06-08→2026-06-28), the **field_shown → coupon_applied rate is 27.62%** (580/2,100). This is now a **verified set-membership join** (per known_issues.md D1 — never `windowFunnel` on this data), not a row-count ratio: all 580 `coupon_applied` and all 268 `coupon_rejected` `user_id`s are a confirmed subset of `coupon_field_shown`'s 2,100 (100% nested), and of the 848 users who got as far as `coupon_entered`, the applied/rejected split is an **exact, non-overlapping partition** — 580 + 268 = 848 with 0 users appearing in both outcomes.

**Valid vs. rejected mix** (of the 848 submitted codes): **68.40% valid** (580 applied) vs. **31.60% rejected** (268 rejected).

**Top reject reasons** (of 268 rejections, `coupon_rejected.reject_reason`):
| Reason | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| `min_cart_not_met` | 80 | 29.85% |
| `already_used` | 75 | 27.99% |
| `expired` | 60 | 22.39% |
| `invalid_code` | 53 | 19.78% |

No reason dominates — all four sit within a ~10pp band, led narrowly by cart-value-threshold misses and code-reuse attempts.

**Scope/caveats:** sample is the full loaded Promo/Coupon spec, 2,100 `coupon_field_shown` rows, 2026-06-08 06:00 → 2026-06-28 23:11 (no baseline-funnel window to compare against). Per known_issues.md D2, this spec's `application_id` has 0% overlap with `application_started` — the coupon flow cannot be joined to the main visa funnel and is analyzed standalone here (all joins above used `user_id`, which is safe and confirms the funnel is perfectly nested and non-overlapping — the first live-query confirmation for spec 06, no `analysis/qNN.md` existed for it before this file). `coupon_code` breakdowns (e.g. `EXPIRED5` accounting for 55.6% of rejections but never appearing on `coupon_applied`) and discount economics were not part of this question and are left to a dedicated coupon-code/margin question.
