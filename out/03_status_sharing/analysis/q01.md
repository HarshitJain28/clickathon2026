## Question
Share rate, and does it vary by `status_shared` (do approvals get shared more)?

## Answer
There is no independent count of applications-per-status anywhere in the loaded
tables (`status_shared` is only recorded on the 3 sharer-side Status Sharing
tables, and this spec's `application_id` has confirmed 0% overlap with
`application_started` — D2), so a "share rate" can't be computed as shares ÷
total applications at each status. The rate that *can* be computed honestly is
the **share-flow completion rate**: of everyone who clicks "share"
(`share_clicked`), what fraction actually finishes the flow (selects a channel
and gets a link generated). Computed by set-membership join on `share_id`
(per known_issues.md D1 — not `windowFunnel`), this is **71.5% overall
(1,144 / 1,600)**, and it is essentially flat across `status_shared`:

| `status_shared` | Shares (`share_clicked`) | Completed (`channel_selected` = `link_generated`) | Completion rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| submitted | 562 (35.1%) | 394 | 70.11% |
| approved | 513 (32.1%) | 365 | 71.15% |
| processing | 525 (32.8%) | 385 | 73.33% |

**No — approvals do not get shared/completed more.** The three statuses split
the 1,600 total shares almost evenly (35/33/32%), and completion rates sit
within a 3.2pp band (70.1%–73.3%) with no monotonic pattern — `processing`,
not `approved`, is actually highest, and the gap is well within what 513–562-row
samples can produce by chance. `channel_selected` and `link_generated` are
confirmed to hold the *exact same 1,144 `share_id`s* in every status bucket
(counts match exactly), confirming the 1:1 pairing between those two tables
flagged in `tables/index.md`.

**Scope/caveats:** Status Sharing sample, 1,600 `share_clicked` rows,
2026-06-08 06:00 → 2026-07-01 09:21. This is the first `analysis/qNN.md` for
spec 03, so it also newly verifies (via `share_id` set membership) the
step-through row-count ratio (71.5%) that `tables/index.md` previously
flagged as unverified. Per D2, this whole flow is standalone — it cannot be
joined to `application_started` or the main funnel, so this "share rate" is
scoped to the share flow itself, not applications overall.
