## Question
Channel mix, and which channel drives the most **new-user** opens?

## Answer
**WhatsApp dominates channel mix and drives the most new-user opens, by a wide margin.**

Channel mix at the sharer's channel-selection step (`channel_selected`, 1,144 rows, one row per user/share):

| Channel | Selections | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| whatsapp | 625 | 54.63% |
| copy_link | 214 | 18.71% |
| email | 194 | 16.96% |
| sms | 111 | 9.70% |

New-user opens by channel, measured directly on the recipient-side `link_opened` table (2,310 rows, `recipient_is_new_user` flag; this is opens, not distinct shares — a link can be reopened, see caveat below):

| Channel | Total opens | New-user opens | New-user rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **whatsapp** | 1,273 | **783** | 61.51% |
| copy_link | 423 | 254 | 60.05% |
| email | 396 | 233 | 58.84% |
| sms | 218 | 120 | 55.05% |

WhatsApp drives the most new-user opens both in absolute terms (783 of 1,390 total new-user opens, 56.3%) and has the *highest* new-user rate of the four channels (61.51%), so it isn't just winning on volume — it's also the most efficient channel per open at pulling in people who are new to the platform. The channel ranking is consistent across both the sharer-side selection step and the recipient-side open step.

**Scope/caveats:** Status Sharing (spec 03) sample, 2026-06-08→2026-07-01, 1,600 shares / 1,144 channel selections / 2,310 opens. `link_opened` carries its own `channel` column directly (no `application_id`; recipient-side, no `user_id` — per known_issues.md D2/D6), so this used a single-table group-by and did not need the still-unverified sharer-side↔recipient-side `share_id` join (per known_issues.md D1, no `analysis/qNN.md` had verified that join before this one). "Opens" here counts every open event, not distinct recipients/shares — 2,310 opens span only 922 distinct `share_id`s, so some reopening by the same link is included; a "distinct shares with a new-user open" cut would read a bit lower but the channel ranking is very unlikely to flip given the size of WhatsApp's lead. `application_id` on this spec's tables does not join `application_started` (0% overlap, D2) — this is a standalone-flow finding, not tied to the main funnel.
