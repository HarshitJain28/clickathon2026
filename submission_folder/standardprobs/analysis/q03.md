## Question
Visualize regressions or trends over the last quarter?

## Answer
Scope: "last quarter" = Q2 2026 (2026-04-01 → 2026-06-30), the last full calendar quarter as of today (2026-08-02) and fully within the live data window. Queried live against `application_started`, `purchase_completed`, `document_uploaded`, `pay_now_clicked`, `destination_card_clicked` in `clickathon` (2026-08-02). Funnel conversion was computed by set-membership join on `user_id`, per known_issues.md D1 (not `windowFunnel`, which loses ~52% of conversions on this data).

**Headline regression — Android document-capture failures, up ~5x (K2, reconfirmed):** weekly `is_crossed_failed_attempt_threshold` rate on `document_uploaded` rose almost every week of Q2 — 7.4% (Apr 5) → 10.6% → 9.1% → 19.6% → 21.5% → 23.8% → 22.8% → 27.4% → 30.9% → 32.4% → 30.0% → 34.3% (Jun 21), monthly 11.69%→23.43%→33.54%. iOS stayed flat all quarter (7.5%–10.8%, monthly 8.33%/8.09%/10.16%). This is an exact live reproduction of known_issues.md → K2, still accelerating in the most recent week measured. Caveat (D3): this flag doesn't reliably track true capture quality elsewhere in the data (71.4% of flagged events have zero retries), so the *trend* is robust but the *absolute level* carries some doubt.

**Trend — funnel conversion kept sliding:** application→purchase conversion fell from ~4.9–5.1% in early April to ~3.7–4.0% by June (monthly: 4.92% Apr → 4.49% May → 3.96% Jun), continuing the broad H1 2026 decline already on record (business.md: 4.91%→3.93%, Jan→Jun) — present in essentially every week of Q2, not a one-month blip. This dataset can't attribute a cause to it (K4 Schengen-seasonality and K6 SUMMER20-campaign explanations are both refuted elsewhere in known_issues.md).

**Not a regression — funnel volume:** indexing each stage to its Apr-5 week (=100), top-of-funnel traffic (card clicks, applications) grew a steady ~18–20% across the quarter — healthy organic growth. But `pay_now_clicked` and `purchase_completed` volumes stayed roughly flat, dipping below their April baseline in several weeks — the flip side of the declining conversion rate: more people entering the funnel, a shrinking share coming out the other end.

Sample/scope: 12 full ISO weeks (Apr 5 – Jun 27 2026; the partial first/last calendar weeks of the query window were excluded from the weekly charts), ~85,000 applications and ~3,500 purchases in Q2. See the full report with charts: q03_report.html.
