## Question
Where are we losing conversions, and for which segments (device / geo / destination) visualize it based on segments

## Answer
Live query against `clickathon` (H1 2026 main funnel, `destination_card_clicked` → `application_started` → `document_uploaded` → `pay_now_clicked` → `purchase_completed`, computed by **set membership** per `known_issues.md` D1, not `windowFunnel`, which would understate late-funnel loss by 52%) confirms the two real leaks and shows they are **not evenly distributed across device, geo, or destination**.

**Where the loss happens (stage-level, all segments combined):**
- `application_started → document_uploaded`: **86.76% drop** (133,967 users lost) — the single largest absolute leak, and it is essentially **flat across every device type** (13.0%–13.6% step-through everywhere), so this leak is a product/UX problem, not a segment problem.
- `pay_now_clicked → purchase_completed`: **52.14% drop** (7,685 users lost) — the second-biggest leak, and this is where almost all the segment variation concentrates.

**By device** (`funnel_conversion` = purchased ÷ started; n = 154,413 started):
| Device | Started | Purchased | Funnel conversion | pay→purchase rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| iOS | 63,520 | 3,193 | **5.03%** (best) | 49.88% |
| Desktop | 10,655 | 499 | 4.68% | 46.51% |
| web-user-b2c | 30,611 | 1,335 | 4.36% | 46.08% |
| Android | 49,627 | 2,027 | **4.08%** (worst) | 46.41% |

~1pp spread, driven almost entirely at the payment step (iOS 49.9% vs. Android 46.4%), not the doc-upload step. Note: `known_issues.md` K1 ("Gulf/iOS pay-step abandonment") is refuted — iOS is consistently the *best*-converting platform here, consistent with K1's prior refutation on this same funnel.

**By geo** (`geoip_country_code`, top 10 by volume):
| Geo | Started | Purchased | Funnel conversion | pay→purchase rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AE | 21,630 | 1,163 | **5.38%** (best) | 56.24% |
| QA | 3,163 | 152 | 4.81% | 46.77% |
| GB | 6,183 | 293 | 4.74% | 49.16% |
| OM | 2,581 | 119 | 4.61% | 51.74% |
| OTHER | 13,600 | 623 | 4.58% | 45.08% |
| AU | 5,949 | 277 | 4.66% | 46.79% |
| SA | 4,716 | 212 | 4.50% | 48.74% |
| IN | 86,506 | 3,791 | 4.38% | 46.18% |
| US | 7,756 | 338 | 4.36% | 48.70% |
| SG | 2,329 | 86 | **3.69%** (worst) | 41.35% |

~1.7pp spread (AE 5.38% vs. SG 3.69%), again mostly explained by the pay→purchase step (AE 56.2% vs. SG 41.4%). India, the dominant volume geo (86,506 started, 56% of all applications), sits mid-pack at 4.38%.

**By destination** (27 values, `application_started → purchase_completed`):
Best: QA (**5.69%**, n=1,651), FR (5.29%), OM (5.19%). Worst: KR (**4.11%**, n=1,192), ID (4.23%), TH (4.31%). Spread is ~1.6pp — modest and with no obvious regional clustering (Gulf destinations span both the best, QA, and only-mid, AE at 4.40%).

**Bottom line:** the biggest, actionable leak (application → document upload, 86.76%) is uniform across segments — fixing it is a product change, not a targeting one. The smaller pay→purchase leak (52.14%) is where device/geo/destination actually matter, with a consistent ~1.5–2pp spread everywhere, and no known issue in the wiki (K1, K4 checked and refuted) explains it — it lines up with the unexplained 20% relative decline in overall `funnel_conversion` across H1 2026 (4.91%→3.93%) noted in `metrics/funnel_conversion.md`.

Scope: H1 2026 baseline data (2025-12-31→2026-07-01), 1,000,000 card-clicks → 7,054 purchases, all counts by exact `uniqExact(user_id)` set membership (funnel is perfectly nested, so no join was required for the un-segmented counts). See the full report: q02_report.html.
