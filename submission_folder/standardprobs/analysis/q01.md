## Question
Analyze the existing funnel and surface the most important issues, with the why.

## Answer

Scope: the core visa funnel — `destination_card_clicked → application_started → document_uploaded → pay_now_clicked → purchase_completed` — over the full H1 2026 baseline window (2025-12-31 23:41 → 2026-07-01, 1,000,000 users at the top). All numbers below were re-queried live just now by set membership (never `windowFunnel`/timestamp order — see D1: naive time-ordered funnel tools understate conversion by 52%, reporting 2.18% instead of the true 4.57%).

**Live-confirmed funnel:**

| Stage | Users | Step-through |
|---|---:|---:|
| destination_card_clicked | 1,000,000 | — |
| application_started | 154,413 | 15.44% |
| document_uploaded | 20,446 | **13.24%** |
| pay_now_clicked | 14,739 | 72.09% |
| purchase_completed | 7,054 | 47.86% |

Card→purchase: 0.71%. **Application→purchase (the default `funnel_conversion` metric): 4.57%.**

### Issue 1 — The single biggest leak is application_started → document_uploaded (86.8% loss)

13.24% step-through dwarfs every other stage's loss — bigger than the payment-step leak (47.86% step-through) and the initial browse-to-apply leak (15.44%) combined in relative terms. This is where the majority of "applications" simply disappear, and `base_context.md` does not mention it at all.

### Issue 2 — The H1 portfolio-wide conversion decline is real, and now localized to one specific step

Application→purchase conversion fell from 4.94% (Jan) to 3.96% (June), a ~20% relative drop, confirmed live by month:

| Month | Applications | Purchases | Conv % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-01 | 21,580 | 1,066 | 4.94% |
| 2026-02 | 21,211 | 1,000 | 4.71% |
| 2026-03 | 25,298 | 1,167 | 4.61% |
| 2026-04 | 26,280 | 1,292 | 4.92% |
| 2026-05 | 29,665 | 1,331 | 4.49% |
| 2026-06 | 30,370 | 1,204 | 3.96% |

Decomposing by stage (all queried live) shows the decline is **not** coming from the payment step, which is flat all year (pay_now_clicked→purchase_completed: 49.8% Jan → 47.6% June, no trend), and only mildly from the top of the funnel (application_started→document_uploaded: 13.3% Jan → 12.4% June). It is concentrated in **document_uploaded → pay_now_clicked**, which erodes steadily from **74.5% (Jan) to 67.1% (June)** — a real ~7pp fall. That timing lines up with the known Android passport-capture regression (K2, re-verified live below), which also inflects in April and keeps worsening through June — a plausible, though not proven, contributing driver (K2's quality flag is measured on document_uploaded rows themselves, not a direct predictor of who proceeds to pay, so this is a correlational, not causal, link). Three alternative explanations for the broad decline were tested previously and refuted: Schengen summer scarcity (K4 — Schengen isn't distinctly worse), the 7.45 app rollout (K7 — `app_version` carries no real temporal signal), and the SUMMER20 campaign (K6 — it's an always-on code, not seasonal).

### Issue 3 — Android passport-capture failures are accelerating (K2), re-verified live and still the highest-priority finding

| Month | Android fail % | iOS fail % |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-01 | 6.64% | 8.21% |
| 2026-02 | 9.33% | 9.08% |
| 2026-03 | 7.88% | 8.28% |
| 2026-04 | 12.05% | 8.33% |
| 2026-05 | 23.07% | 8.09% |
| 2026-06 | **33.76%** | 10.16% |

Android's failure rate rose ~5x since April while iOS stayed flat — by June, roughly 1 in 3 Android document-capture attempts is flagged. `base_context.md` frames this as "some devices... being monitored"; the live data says it's a severe, still-worsening regression concentrated in exactly the step (document upload) driving Issue 2. Caveat: the underlying flag (`is_crossed_failed_attempt_threshold`) is internally inconsistent (D3 — 71.4% of flagged events had zero retries), so the absolute rate should be read with caution, but the trend (monotonic, Android-only, correctly timed) is robust.

### Issue 4 — pay_now_clicked → purchase_completed still loses 52% of payment intents, with no confirmed explanation

This step is large (14,739 → 7,054) and stable, not improving. The one candidate theory in `base_context.md` — an iOS/WebKit OTP regression hitting Gulf card users hardest (K1) — is refuted on this funnel: iOS is the best-converting high-volume platform (49.88% vs. Android 46.77%), and in the UAE specifically iOS beats Android by 27pp, the opposite of the claim. (A narrower, real iOS-only OTP-step regression was separately confirmed in the standalone Express Checkout flow, but that cannot be joined to this main funnel — see known_issues.md D2 — so it does not explain this leak.) This remains a large, real, and currently unattributed loss.

### Issue 5 — A silent, un-analyzed cohort exists before the funnel even starts

Live-confirmed: of 183,790 users who complete `auth_completed`, **29,377 (16.0%) never start an application at all.** `auth_completed` is a superset of the funnel's first analyzed stage, not a peer of it, and this cohort — people who log in but never even begin applying — sits entirely outside every dashboard built on `application_started` onward.

### Bottom line

Ranked by importance: (1) the 86.8% application→document-upload leak is the single largest drop in absolute terms; (2) the H1 conversion decline is real and now traced specifically to the document-upload→pay-click step, plausibly linked to (3) the accelerating Android capture-quality regression; (4) the 52% payment-intent leak remains large and unexplained by any tested theory; (5) 29,377 authenticated-but-never-applied users are an unaddressed pre-funnel cohort. Any funnel tooling must count by set membership, not `windowFunnel`, or conversion will read as 2.18% instead of the true 4.57%.
